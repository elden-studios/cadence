# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 27 May 2026
**Publisher:** Elden Studios
**Contact:** bazerbashi@elden-studios.com

Cadence is built privacy-first. This policy describes what limited information we and our service providers handle when you use the Cadence iOS application ("App"), and what we do not.

## TL;DR

- We **do not** operate a server. We **do not** receive your time entries, clients, invoices, or business profile.
- Your data lives in **your own iCloud account**, synced by Apple's CloudKit framework. We cannot read it.
- We **do not** use analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking of any kind.
- Subscriptions are handled by Apple's App Store. We see only what the App Store reports to us (a subscription receipt; we do not see your payment information).
- We do not sell, share, or rent any data to third parties.

## What we collect — directly

**Nothing.** The App does not connect to any server we operate. We have no servers, no databases, no analytics pipelines, no error-reporting service, and no advertising integrations. If you uninstall the App without contacting us, we will have no record that you ever used it.

## What is handled by Apple on your behalf

The following data is processed by Apple (not by us) when you use the App:

### iCloud / CloudKit storage

When you create clients, projects, time entries, invoices, or your business profile, those records are stored in your own Apple iCloud account using CloudKit. This data:

- Lives in your iCloud and is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and [iCloud Terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/icloud/).
- Is encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple.
- Is **not visible to us**. We have no developer-side access to your CloudKit data.
- Is synced across your own Apple devices that are signed into the same Apple ID and have iCloud Drive enabled for the App.

If you sign out of iCloud or disable iCloud Drive for the App, the App will continue to work locally on the device but will not sync to your other devices.

### App Store / StoreKit subscriptions

If you start a Cadence Pro free trial or purchase a subscription, Apple's App Store handles the transaction. Apple shares a **subscription receipt** with the App. The receipt tells us whether your subscription is active, when it renews, and which product you purchased. It does **not** include your name, email, payment method, or any other personal information.

App Store transactions are governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

### Apple's standard system services

Like every iOS app, Cadence uses standard Apple system frameworks. These may include:

- **Local notifications** (UNUserNotificationCenter) — for invoice reminders and recurring-invoice catch-up alerts. Scheduled and delivered entirely on your device.
- **Siri / Shortcuts** (AppIntents) — if you choose to use the "Start timer" intent.
- **Live Activities & widgets** — running-timer display on the Lock Screen and Home Screen.

None of these connect to any server we control.

## What we do not do

- We do not collect analytics, telemetry, or crash reports.
- We do not use third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, attribution, or fingerprinting.
- We do not have ads, in any form, anywhere in the App.
- We do not sell, share, rent, or otherwise disclose your data to third parties.
- We do not track you across other apps or websites.

## Your rights

Because we have no copy of your data, most rights typically described in privacy policies (access, deletion, portability) apply to your **iCloud account**, which you control directly:

- **Access / export:** invoices can be exported as PDFs from within the App. With a Cadence Pro subscription, time entries can be exported as CSV.
- **Deletion:** delete the App from your device and remove its iCloud data from **Settings → [your Apple ID] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Cadence**.
- **Subscription data:** request copies of your transaction history from your Apple ID account at [appleid.apple.com](https://appleid.apple.com).

If you contact us at the address below, we will help you to the extent we can — but in nearly all cases, the action you need is on Apple's side, not ours.

## Children

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the App in a way that requires action, please contact us.

## International users

Because we do not collect personal data ourselves, the App does not transfer your personal data internationally. Apple's processing of your iCloud data and App Store transactions is governed by Apple's own privacy practices and the laws applicable to Apple.

If you are in the European Union, the United Kingdom, California, or another jurisdiction with specific privacy laws (such as GDPR, CCPA, or the Personal Data Protection Law of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia), the rights you have under those laws apply to whatever party actually holds your personal data — which, in nearly all cases, is **you** (in your own iCloud account) or **Apple** (in its App Store records), not us.

## Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent change. We will not retroactively reduce protections we have already given you.
