# Cadence

**Track hours. Send invoices.** Made for freelancers and consultants.

Cadence is a native iOS time tracker and invoice generator. Start a timer in one tap, build polished PDF invoices from your tracked hours, and see exactly where your time and earnings went.

## What's included

- One-tap timer with smart Switch and Resume
- Unlimited clients and projects
- Beautifully formatted PDF invoices with your branding
- Recurring invoices for monthly retainers
- Payment reminders that nag the client so you don't have to
- 150+ currencies, including SAR, AED, USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, INR, and many more
- iCloud sync across your iPhone
- Live Activities, Lock Screen widgets, and Siri Shortcuts
- Privacy by design — your data lives in **your** iCloud, not on any of our servers
